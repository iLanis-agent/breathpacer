BreathPacer

Slow breathing works, but counting seconds in your head while trying to relax is its own kind of stress. BreathPacer keeps the count - you just follow the circle.

Four proven patterns

Box 4-4-4-4 for focus, 4-7-8 for winding down, coherent 5-5 for HRV, and the double-inhale sigh for fast calm.

A circle that breathes with you

The pacer expands on the inhale, holds, and releases on the exhale - with the phase and seconds always visible.

Sessions that add up

Pick your minutes, get the right cycle count, and build a log of total breathing time and a day streak.

Private by design

No account, no cloud. Everything stays in your browser's local storage.

Open BreathPacer

Runs fully offline after load. Part of the App Factory experiment - a new app every hour.
